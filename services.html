Our Services

The Philippine National Police offers a wide range of services to ensure the safety and security of the Filipino people. Browse through our service offerings and discover how we can assist you in your needs.

Available Services

Firearms and Explosives Services

The Firearms and Explosives Office (FEO) manages all services related to firearms ownership, registration, and permits. We ensure proper regulation and control of firearms and explosives to maintain public safety.

  • License to Own and Possess Firearms (LTOPF)
  • Firearm Registration and Renewal
  • Permits for Firearms and Ammunition Purchase, Transport, and Use
  • Permits for Explosives and Fireworks
Visit the FEO website for detailed information and online applications

Forensic and Crime Laboratory Services

The PNP Forensic Group provides scientific and technical investigative support to law enforcement agencies. Our expert teams use advanced technology and methods to analyze evidence and help solve crimes.

  • DNA Analysis
  • Fingerprint Examination
  • Ballistics and Firearms Identification
  • Scene of the Crime Operations (SOCO)
  • Medico-Legal Services
Learn more at the PNP Forensic Group website

Medical and Health Services

The PNP Medical Records Management System (MRMS) offers comprehensive healthcare services for PNP personnel and their dependents. Our digital platform streamlines access to healthcare services and medical records.

  • Outpatient Consultations
  • Medical Appointments and Scheduling
  • Health Records Management
Access these services through the PNP MRMS portal

Training and Development

The PNP is committed to continuous improvement through comprehensive training programs and resources for personnel development. Our training modules are designed to enhance skills and capabilities of our officers.

  • Physical Fitness Tests
  • Marksmanship Training
  • Instructor Pool Management
Details are available on the SISTIMS platform

Specialized Operational Units

The PNP has established several specialized units to address specific security challenges. These units are equipped with specialized training, tools, and methodologies to handle their respective areas of responsibility.

  • Criminal Investigation and Detection Group (CIDG)
  • Anti-Cybercrime Group (ACG)
  • Drug Enforcement Group (DEG)
  • Special Action Force (SAF)
  • Highway Patrol Group (HPG)
  • Maritime Group
More information on the PNP's organizational structure page

Child Protection Services

The PNP is committed to protecting children from abuse and exploitation. We have established comprehensive policies and procedures to ensure the safety and welfare of children across the nation.

  • Reporting Mechanisms for Child Abuse
  • Protection for Whistleblowers
  • Coordination with Child Welfare Agencies
Refer to the PNP Child Protection Policy document
