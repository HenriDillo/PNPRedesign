Schedule an Appointment

The Philippine National Police offers online scheduling for various services to better serve the public. Choose from the options below to schedule your appointment

How to Schedule an Appointment

Follow these steps to successfully schedule your appointment:

  1. Select the appropriate service category from the options below.
  2. Fill out the appointment form with all required information.
  3. Choose your preferred date and time for the appointment.
  4. Submit your appointment request.
  5. You will receive a confirmation email with your appointment details.
  6. Present your appointment confirmation and valid ID when you arrive at the PNP office.

Note: Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment. Bring all necessary documents and valid identification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I miss my appointment?+

If you miss your appointment, you will need to reschedule. Please note that repeated no-shows may affect your ability to schedule future appointments.

How far in advance can I schedule an appointment?+

You can schedule appointments up to 30 days in advance. We recommend booking at least 3-5 business days before your preferred date.

What documents should I bring to my appointment?+

Please bring a valid government-issued ID, the appointment confirmation email, and any other documents specific to your service requirement.

Can I reschedule my appointment?+

Yes, you can reschedule your appointment up to 24 hours before the scheduled time through our online system or by calling our hotline.

Are there walk-in services available?+

Limited walk-in services are available, but priority is given to scheduled appointments. We strongly recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure prompt service.
